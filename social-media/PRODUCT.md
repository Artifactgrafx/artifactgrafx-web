# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Small business owners who are overwhelmed by tech, don't know where to start with their online presence, and need someone to help them get online without the agency speak or complicated process.

## Product Purpose
Artifact Grafx is a brand excavation service that helps small businesses discover their existing brand, polish it, and bring it online. One conversation, one design, one launch at a time.

## Brand Personality
- Archaeological/excavation themed (artcheologist, artifacts, dig)
- Direct and no-nonsense
- Warm but not overly casual
- Confident and grounded
- Mystical/archaeological without being pretentious

## Anti-references
- Generic agency speak and buzzwords
- Overcomplicated 50-page decks
- Cookie-cutter template sites
- Overly playful/cute branding
- Corporate cold/sterile aesthetic

## Design Principles
- **Excavate, don't invent**: Find what's already there in the business, don't force a personality that doesn't exist
- **Simple over complex**: One conversation, one design, one launch. No overwhelming process
- **Substance over flash**: Quality craftsmanship over trendy effects
- **Authentic over generic**: Every brand should feel uniquely like the business it represents
- **Accessible guidance**: Meet small business owners where they are, especially when they're tech overwhelmed

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance where possible
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Sufficient color contrast
- Semantic HTML
- Clear, readable typography
