# Thought-Provoking Quotes for ArtifactGrafx IG

*Post these as quote cards. No sales pitch. Let the aesthetic and ideas draw people in. The link in bio is the case study.*

## Art & Creativity

### Quote 1: "Create"
```
CREATE
before you're ready.
CREATE
before it's perfect.
CREATE
because you have to.
```
*Style: Bold stacked text, minimal, gold accent on "CREATE"*

### Quote 2: "The Sketchbook"
```
A sketchbook is not a portfolio.
It's a graveyard of ideas
and a laboratory for the next one.
```
*Style: Typographic, editorial feel*

### Quote 3: "References"
```
Good artists borrow.
Great artists steal with intent.
```
*Style: Classic quote, punchy*

### Quote 4: "Messy First"
```
The first mark is always ugly.
The hundredth starts to look like you.
```
*Style: Conversational, relatable*

## Business & Craft

### Quote 8: "Craft"
```
Craft is care
repeated long enough
to become instinct.
```
*Style: Philosophical, applies to all creative work*

### Quote 9: "Excavate"
```
Most things worth keeping
are already there.
You just have to look harder.
```
*Style: Subtly ties to brand without pitching*

### Quote 10: "Taste"
```
Taste is knowing
what to keep
and having the discipline
to leave the rest.
```
*Style: Very shareable among designers/business owners*

### Quote 11: "Process"
```
The work you do
when no one's watching

```
*Style: Motivational without being cheesy*

### Quote 12: "Time"
```
Speed is a skill.
Restraint is mastery.
```
*Style: Minimal, impactful*

## Posting Strategy

**Approach:**
- Post 4-6x/week of pure quotes/thoughts
- No "link in bio", no "DM me", no service mentions
- Focus on saves and shares - these are bookmarkable
- Use the same aesthetic as your brand (dark bg, grid, gold accents)
- Let curiosity build over 2-3 months
- People will naturally click profile, see the cohesive aesthetic, check link in bio

**Best Performers:**
- Quote 10 "Taste" - resonates strongly with designers and business owners
- Quote 3 "Great artists steal with intent" - classic, highly shareable
- Quote 8 "Craft is care repeated..." - universal appeal
- Quote 12 "Speed is a skill. Restraint is mastery." - minimalist, very saveable

**Engagement:**
- Don't ask for likes/comments aggressively
- If someone asks "who are you?" in comments, engage naturally
- Use subtle Story posts to show process/work without pitching
- Let the IG page BE the portfolio/case study as you intended
