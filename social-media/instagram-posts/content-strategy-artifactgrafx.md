# ArtifactGrafx Instagram Content Strategy

*For @artifactgrafx - Brand Excavation + Art & Design Showcase*

## Brand Positioning

**Tagline:** "Discover. Design. Deliver."  
**Mission:** "Excavate. Polish. Bring it online."  
**Vibe:** Archaeological meets creative studio - where design history, graffiti, music, and craftsmanship collide.

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Brand Excavation (Business-Focused)
*Goal: Attract small business owners who need branding/web design*

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------------|-----------|---------|
| "Buried Brand" posts | 2x/week | Showcase the excavation process |
| Client spotlights ("Unearthed") | 1x/week | Social proof (use your "Unearthed Artifacts" concept) |
| Before/After reveals | 1x/week | Show transformation |
| Process posts | 1x/week | Educate on branding process |

### Pillar 2: Art & Graphics Showcase
*Goal: Build a creative portfolio, attract design-minded audience*

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------------|-----------|---------|
| Logo studies/explorations | 2-3x/week | Showcase skills and process |
| Type studies | 2x/week | Typography exploration |
| Experimental graphics | 1-2x/week | Show range and creativity |
| Designer spotlights | 1x/week | Community building |

### Pillar 3: Street Culture & Music
*Goal: Add personality, reach broader creative audience*

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------------|-----------|---------|

| Vinyl/art crossover | 1x/week | Album art appreciation |
| "Inspo from the streets" | 1x/week | Cultural references |

### Pillar 4: Creative Mindset
*Goal: High shareability, build community*

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------------|-----------|---------|
| Motivational quotes | 3-4x/week | Your "creativity sparked" quote style |
| Behind the scenes | 1-2x/week | Process, studio/workflow |

### Grid Aesthetic
- **Color palette:** Stick to your dark theme (#1f1b17) with gold accents (#d9a95b)
- **Mix ratio:** 40% business/brand content, 30% art/showcase, 30% culture/music/mindset
- **Consistency:** Use same filter/treatment or keep raw. Lean into the archaeological/grid aesthetic
- **Storytelling:** Treat grid like a digital sketchbook that also serves as portfolio

## Growth Strategy

1. **Study accounts:** Follow and engage with great designers, graffiti artists, jazz vinyl accounts, and type lovers
2. **Engage authentically:** Comment on posts in street art, jazz, hip hop communities
3. **Use location tags:** Tag local spots, studios when relevant
4. **Story engagement:** Polls ("Jazz or Hip Hop?"), quizzes, Q&As about design
5. **Cross-pollinate:** Your business content will reach creatives, your art content will reach business owners who appreciate craft
6. **Show process:** Time-lapses of lettering/logo work perform extremely well
7. **Lean into personality:** The "artcheologist" angle is unique - own it

## Content Creation Tools

- **Graphics:** Create in same style as your website (dark bg, grid, gold accents, Space Grotesk)
- **Reels:** Film hand lettering, logo sketches, or design process
- **Editing:** Keep gritty/authentic for street content, polished for business content
- **Music:** Use trending jazz/lo-fi hip hop for reels (check rights or use Instagram audio)
